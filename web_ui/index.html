The Clinical Imperative

Why PulmoTwin? The Case, The Need & The Impact

Transforming conventional reactive asthma management into proactive, AI-guided attack prevention.

The Clinical Problem (The Need)

Over 300 million individuals globally suffer from chronic bronchial asthma. Clinical evidence reveals that over 80% of severe hospitalizations and acute attacks are triggered by undetected ambient air pollution spikes (PM2.5, NO2, SO2) interacting with hyper-reactive airways. Conventional asthma management is purely reactive—patients take medication only after airway constriction has already begun.

The PulmoTwin Breakthrough (The Case)

PulmoTwin bridges continuous satellite and ground-station IoT environmental telemetry with validated clinical guidelines (GINA criteria). By applying collaborative multimodal machine learning (combining Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting), the system predicts elevated exacerbation risk hours before symptom onset, giving patients a critical preventive window.

The Patient & Physician Impact (The Benefits)

Patients receive personalized, actionable care directives, real-time smart inhaler canister tracking, and immediate emergency SOS dispatch during acute flare-ups. Clinicians gain objective, longitudinal population telemetry, verifiable medication adherence records, and calibrated risk scores backed by deterministic medical safety rails.

The Indian Epidemiological Emergency

India's Escalating Asthma Crisis & Urban Smog Reality

India represents the highest asthma fatality epicenter worldwide: according to The Lancet Global Health, India accounts for 42.4% of all global asthma deaths despite having only 11.1% of global cases. In Indian megacities (Delhi-NCR, Kanpur, Kolkata, Mumbai, Lucknow), winter ambient PM2.5 levels routinely cross 250 to 450+ μg/m³—up to 30 times the WHO safe threshold. PulmoTwin was designed specifically to give Indian citizens proactive early warning before toxic particulate spikes trigger irreversible acute attacks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PulmoTwin's early-warning models, PulmoTwin agent decisions, and clinical safety standards.

What is PulmoTwin and how does it predict asthma attacks?

PulmoTwin is an AI-powered clinical decision support platform designed specifically for Indian urban realities. It continuously correlates local environmental sensor telemetry (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, temperature, and humidity) with personal patient risk markers and medication adherence. Using an ensemble of machine learning models (XGBoost, Random Forest, and LightGBM), it predicts personal exacerbation risk hours before airway constriction begins.

How does the PulmoTwin autonomous agent work?

The PulmoTwin agent acts as an autonomous proactive co-pilot. It operates on a continuous feedback loop: assessing environmental hazard forecasts, validating canister dose counts from smart inhalers, checking medical safety guardrails (aligned with GINA guidelines), and generating tiered interventions—from pre-emptive dosage reminders to emergency SOS alerts.

Why is PulmoTwin specifically tailored for Indian cities?

According to The Lancet Global Health, India accounts for 42.4% of all global asthma deaths despite representing only 11.1% of global asthma cases. In major Indian megacities (Delhi-NCR, Kanpur, Kolkata, Mumbai, Lucknow), winter PM2.5 levels routinely cross 250 to 450+ μg/m³—over 30 times the WHO safe limit. PulmoTwin was trained on real-world Indian urban meteorological and hospital admission telemetry to protect vulnerable citizens during severe smog spikes.

Does PulmoTwin formulate medical diagnoses or change my prescription?

No. PulmoTwin is an assistive early-warning and decision support system, not an autonomous medical diagnostic device. It operates under deterministic clinical safety guardrails: it never alters physician-prescribed dosage amounts and always defers to licensed clinicians. In acute emergencies, it provides immediate one-tap connectivity to national emergency services (112 / 108).

How does smart inhaler tracking and dose management work?

Patients can log their doses manually or connect smart inhaler sensor sleeves via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). The system maintains an accurate longitudinal record of remaining canister doses, actuation timestamps, and compliance ratios. When canister reserves drop below 20% or adherence lapses during high-smog forecasts, the agent proactively issues refill warnings.

What happens if a patient experiences sudden acute distress?

If severe respiratory distress is indicated or extreme trigger thresholds are detected, PulmoTwin activates its High-Risk Emergency Protocol. This immediately displays calm, step-by-step rescue inhaler instructions, transmits telemetry to designated family caregivers, and presents direct one-tap emergency calling to 112 / 108.

How is my personal health data secured and protected?

All telemetry, medication logs, and clinical profiles are stored with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Patient data remains strictly segregated from clinician dashboards using role-based access tokens, ensuring full privacy and patient autonomy.
